# Privacy Policy for KeepNote

**Last updated:** 4 March 2026  

KeepNote (“the App”) is developed by Encoder IT (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

### Notes and Content You Create
All notes, reminders, and other content you create in the App are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Your notes and other data are used only inside the App to provide its core functionality (creating, editing, and managing notes).

We do not transmit your notes or personal data to our servers or any third-party servers.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device’s storage.

We recommend that you regularly back up your device and use device-level security features such as screen lock and encryption to protect your data.

If you uninstall the App or clear its data, your locally stored notes may be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by us.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.

The App does not share your data with third parties.

> If you use any third-party services like Firebase, Google Analytics, or AdMob, replace this section with a clear description and links to those services’ privacy policies.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document, with the “Last updated” date adjusted accordingly.
